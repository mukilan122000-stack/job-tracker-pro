📥 How to get your LinkedIn Connections CSV

  1. Go to LinkedIn.com → Profile photo → Settings & Privacy
  2. Click Data Privacy → Get a copy of your data
  3. Select Connections only → Request archive
  4. LinkedIn emails a ZIP file in ~10 minutes
  5. Open ZIP → find Connections.csv → upload below
  6. To refresh after new connections — repeat and upload again
